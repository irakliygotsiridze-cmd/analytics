Key finding: isolated incidents show minimal L2S impact (−0.15 pp). However, when 3 or more incidents cluster on a single day, L2S consistently falls ~1 pp below baseline. The most critical period — April 21–30 — saw auto-distribution, greeting templates, and the L2B funnel fail simultaneously: three systems directly responsible for converting leads into sales. L2S dropped to 4.84% against a baseline of 6.35%, a −24% decline in conversion efficiency.
