⚠ Critical Insight — The Probation Dilution Trap
Raw capacity is solved by Week 2 (189 TCMs = 1,928 att/week vs 1,448–1,583 needed). But capacity ≠ sales output. Probation TCMs convert at 15.5% A2S (weeks 1-2) vs tenured at 32%. If attendance is distributed proportionally, adding 52 probation TCMs drops blended A2S from 32% to 27.5% — producing fewer sales than having no probation at all. The solution: smart allocation (route leads to tenured first) + push tenured to 19 slots so they can absorb FM attendance alone. Probation TCMs serve as safety net and ramp for July.

Recommendation
Execute the Action Plan scenario. The three high-impact interventions are: (1) push tenured TCMs to 19 slots/week by Week 3, (2) route attendance priority to tenured TCMs with smart allocation, and (3) intensive coaching sprint for probation TCMs. This combination delivers ~2,060 sales (97% of target, $577K revenue) even at the realistic 32% A2S rate. The remaining $19K gap is coverable by FU Manager recovery (historically 5-8% of total sales).
If the June-July seasonal A2S boost materializes (as it has in the past 3 years), tenured TCMs reach 35% and we deliver ~2,120 sales — essentially 100% of target ($594K).
The 52 probation TCMs being deployed are not a June play — they are the July capacity investment. By July, these TCMs will have 4+ weeks of experience, expected A2S of 28–30%, and will be critical for sustaining the season's second peak month.
