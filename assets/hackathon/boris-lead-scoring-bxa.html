Part 1 Forecast Accuracy Without L2B

This report tests the two-signal formula: B2Att × Att2S. L2B is excluded from scoring. All validations compare the new formula vs the full three-signal reference.

Key trade-off: removing L2B costs −5.8pp AUC
The B×A formula achieves AUC=0.709 vs 0.767 for the full formula — a meaningful gap. However PB-r vs paid is 0.322 vs 0.356 (−0.034) — a smaller gap in calibration. The practical consequence: 44.9% of leads change segments vs the full formula. Without L2B, leads who register easily (high L2B) but are unlikely to attend/buy are no longer penalised in scoring.

D Cold boundary rises to 1.58% L2S (vs 1.00% in full formula)
Without L2B, D Cold captures leads with low B2Att AND low Att2S — but some of these still register (L2B avg 43.0%). The D Cold floor is higher because moderate-registration leads are no longer dragged down by low L2B. The formula is less discriminating for leads that are 'easy to book but hard to convert'.

Universal patterns hold — but A★ Mid Class signal weakens
Three patterns remain consistent: 1) High Class converts 44–74% across all tiers/channels. 2) Low Class converts 12–39% — wider range without L2B. 3) Mid Class differentiates by tier, but the gap narrows: A★ Mid converges with B Hot Mid more than in the full formula. The loss of L2B means some A★ leads that were strong buyers (high L2B + high B2Att + high Att2S) are now mixed with moderate-L2B leads who share similar B×A scores.
