Patterns tested and dropped

Intellectual honesty: what didn't survive verification

Pattern 5 (Day-of-week scheduling) — DROPPED

v1/v2 found Monday and Friday to be highest-yield Prio days ($78/slot vs $42 on Wed-Thu). This finding was driven entirely by Financial_Strength — a post-attendance variable that correlates with day-of-week for confounding reasons. When re-tested with ABCD segment data, Tuesday-Thursday actually outperform Monday-Friday for Prio TCMs. The pattern was an artifact, not a signal.

Pattern 1 (Lobby waste — Low Class clients filling Prio slots) — DROPPED

v1/v2 claimed 47% of Prio sessions went to Low Class clients with $7.6 revenue. Re-tested with ABCD segment + autodis lobby data: only 613 Prio rows have both ABCD and lobby data. Lobby visit rates are 90%+ across all segments (A★ Star 90.5%, B Hot 94.0%, C Warm 92.7%, D Cold 91.6%). No segment-based lobby waste exists for Prio TCMs.

Data methodology

How these numbers were derived

What the data confirms

  • Segment source: ABCD segment_type field, available from Oct 2025 but substantively populated only from Apr 2026. Analysis uses Apr–May 2026 data (4,208 attended sessions with ABCD tag).
  • TCM tier: Prio defined by TCM name match to the TCM Lifetime sheet (Sheet14: "Performance by Team Mei"). 22 confirmed names; 19 active in Apr–May 2026.
  • Conversion signal: payment_day present = sale made. Revenue_USD is mostly sub-$1 for segment-tagged rows (revenue not yet fully attributed), so A2S is based on payment_day, and AOV uses the 17-month historical average by tier ($259 Prio, $233 Non-Prio).
  • Capacity: 19 active Prio TCMs × 20 max slots/week = 380 max scheduled. 165 observed attended/week = 43% utilisation. User-stated overall utilisation = 60%.
  • Counterfactual: Revenue per redirected A+B session = (Prio A2S on A+B × Prio AOV) − (Non-Prio A2S on A+B × Non-Prio AOV) = $114.3 − $69.3 = $45.0. Multiplied by redirectable sessions per week × 52 weeks.

Known limitations

  • Short observation window: ABCD segment data covers only ~2 months (Apr–May 2026). Seasonal effects (Eid, school breaks) may not be captured. Recommend re-running after 6 months of segment-tagged data.
  • AOV estimated from historical: Prio AOV ($259) and Non-Prio AOV ($233) are from the full Jan 2025–May 2026 dataset. If the AOV gap changes, the counterfactual scales proportionally.
  • Causation vs selection: Prio TCMs may already receive slightly better leads through existing routing logic. The 14.4pp A2S gap could partially be a selection effect. A controlled A/B test (random assignment for 4 weeks) would isolate the true causal TCM effect.
  • Capacity model depends on util assumptions: The conservative $257K assumes the 60% utilisation cap is real. The aggressive $503K assumes it can be pushed to ~87%. The true answer is likely in between.
