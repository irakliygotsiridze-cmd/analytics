Model Methodology
Expected Revenue per Lead = P(conversion | TCM × Course) × E(AOV | TCM × Course)

// P(conversion) = TCM's historical A2S1 rate on this course (min 30 sessions)
// E(AOV) = average order value for this course (from 2025 baseline data)
// Assignment rule: route lead to TCM in top-25% of expected revenue for that course
01
Data Period
2025-01-01 to 2026-05-28. All TCMs active in 2026 are included. Historical 2025 data provides volume for reliable estimates. n ≥ 30 per TCM×Course is required for inclusion.
02
Tiering Logic
Tier 1 (full model): Math Tutoring, Visual Programming, Coding Knight, Graphic Design, Math Master — ≥50 TCMs with reliable data. Tier 2 (marginal): Digital Literacy, Python Start. Fallback: overall TCM A2S used for remaining courses.
03
AOV Source
Revenue_USD from pre-2026 data used as AOV baseline (2026 Revenue fields have a known data quality issue). AOV is fixed per course in the model — not per-TCM, since package upselling variance is absorbed into A2S signal.
04
Uplift Calculation
Uplift = (optimized_a2s − current_a2s) × AOV × attended_volume. Optimized A2S never set below current (floors at baseline). Math Master shows 0% — top-quartile 2025+2026 profile already matches current 2026 performance.
05
Known Limitations
Model assumes TCM performance is stable over time. Does not account for TCM scheduling availability, concurrent session capacity, or new-hire ramp-up curves. Recommend applying recency weighting (2026 data weighted 2×) in production.
06
Next Steps
Integrate with autodistribution system: at lobby entry, route lead to available TCM with highest expected revenue for their enrolled course. Monitor A2S weekly by course. Re-train profiles monthly with rolling 3-month window.

Fleet Overview
⚠ Data coverage note: Completion and lobby metrics are only reliably populated from Feb 2026 onwards — coverage before this date is near-zero. This report uses Feb–May 2026 (4 months). TCM join speed and no-show rate could not be computed from available data (see methodology). Scores should be treated as early signals; re-evaluate quarterly as data matures. System-level failures (autodistribution errors) may artificially lower some TCMs' scores — cross-reference with the tech issues log.
Score Components
40%
Session Completion Rate
Share of assigned sessions with a recorded finish timestamp. Most directly TCM-controlled — measures whether the TCM ran the session to its natural end. Range in data: 0%–97.4%.
35%
Session Duration Score
Median session length normalized against the 90-minute class benchmark. Longer sessions proxy for engagement depth and full pitch delivery. Range: 79–190 min. Capped at 1.0 above 90 min.
25%
Lobby Engagement Rate
Share of assigned sessions where the student entered the lobby. Partially influenced by lead quality and time slot, not purely TCM behavior — hence lowest weight. Range: 54.5%–100%.
