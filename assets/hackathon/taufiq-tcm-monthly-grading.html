Scoring methodology

Why A2S% alone is not enough

A2S% rewards filtering. A TCM with 66% A2S on 7 slots/week = 39 sales. A TCM with 38% A2S on 13 slots/week = 41 sales. The company banks total sales, not percentages. This model combines three dimensions to capture true contribution.

Component 1 — Total Sales (40% weight)
Absolute number of payments generated in the period. Rewards both conversion skill AND volume throughput. Cannot be gamed by running fewer slots.

Component 2 — Revenue per Slot Ran (35% weight)
Formula: (Sales × Historical AOV) / Slots Ran. Captures conversion efficiency AND upselling ability in a single number. A TCM who converts 50% at $300 AOV outscores one who converts 50% at $150 AOV.

Component 3 — Slot Utilisation (25% weight)
Formula: Slots Ran / (Weeks × 20). Penalises under-utilisation. A TCM with great A2S but only 7 slots/week is not contributing at capacity. Note: if ops is not scheduling full 20 slots, this penalises the TCM unfairly — verify with TLs before acting on low-util scores.

Composite Score = Percentile(Sales) × 0.40 + Percentile(Rev/Slot) × 0.35 + Percentile(Util) × 0.25
Each component ranked 0–100 by percentile within the qualified pool. Tiers: Prio ≥75 | Mid 50–74 | Low 25–49 | Probation <25

Reusable prompt

Monthly grading prompt for Head of TCM

Copy-paste this into the analytics portal at the start of each month to get the updated grading.

Use this exact prompt:

Run TCM Monthly Grading for [MONTH YEAR].

Criteria:
- Component 1 (40%): Total Sales = count of attended sessions where payment_day is not null
- Component 2 (35%): Revenue per Slot = (Sales × Historical AOV per TCM) / total happened sessions
- Component 3 (25%): Slot Utilisation = happened sessions / (weeks in period × 20)
- Historical AOV: use each TCMs average Revenue_USD (where ≥$50) from full dataset. If no history, use global avg.
- Qualification: minimum 20 attended sessions in period
- Scoring: percentile rank each component 0-100, then composite = C1×0.40 + C2×0.35 + C3×0.25
- Tiers: Prio ≥75 | Mid 50-74 | Low 25-49 | Probation <25
- Compare vs current tier from TCM Lifetime sheet (Sheet14)

Output:
1. Tier distribution summary (count per tier)
2. Promotion and demotion list vs current Sheet14 assignments
3. Full ranked table: Rank, TCM name, Team, Old tier, New tier, Change, Composite score, Total sales, A2S%, Rev/slot, Util%, Slots/week
4. Flag any TCM with A2S% >40% but Util <30% (possible cherry-picking)
5. Flag any TCM with Util >50% but A2S% <15% (possible quality issue)

Format as downloadable HTML report.
Data notes: Revenue_USD for Apr–May 2026 is mostly sub-$1 (not yet attributed). Sales defined as payment_day present. AOV estimated from full Jan 2025–May 2026 historical data per TCM. 75 additional TCMs were active but below the 20-attended minimum — they appear as "ungraded" and should be reviewed manually by TLs if they are new hires in ramp-up. All findings require review by Danila (Analyst) and Irakly (Analytics Lead).
